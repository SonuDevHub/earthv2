Overview

Why We Recognise

We believe there is no time to lose. Many effective environmental solutions are already delivering results and can be adapted and replicated without reinventing the wheel. Our awards identify, recognise and showcase these proven initiatives so that they can inspire wider action and accelerate meaningful change.

400+

Star Awards and recognitions presented to villages, women's groups, environmental leaders, clean-cooking champions, educational institutions, municipalities and religious sites advancing sustainability.
